Research program

From optimizer geometry to efficient numerical kernels.

The central question is not only which update rule to use, but which structure to capture, how to compute it, and whether the resulting method preserves useful training dynamics.

Research map

Four layers of the optimizer problem.

Model structure

Causal attention, Pre-RMSNorm, RoPE, GQA, SwiGLU, cross-entropy, and residual scaling in modern Transformers.

Second-order objects

Block Hessians, gradient outer products, stochastic-gradient second moments, and their mini-batch decompositions.

Preconditioners

AdamW and Shampoo as distinct transformations of parameter-block geometry and stochastic update statistics.

Matrix functions

Inverse roots, EVD refresh, damping, staleness, polynomial iterations, and GPU utilization.
