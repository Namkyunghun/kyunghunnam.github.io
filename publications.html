FOAM: Frequency and Operator Error-Based Adaptive Damping Method for Reducing Staleness-Oriented Error for Shampoo

Kyunghun Nam, Sumyeong Ahn

FOAM studies stale Shampoo preconditioners through convergence and stability, introduces a proxy for staleness-oriented inverse-preconditioner error, and adaptively controls damping and eigendecomposition frequency to reduce wall-clock overhead while preserving robust training.

Dimension-Independent Convergence Rate for AdaGrad with Heavy-Ball Momentum

Kyunghun Nam, Sejun Park

This paper establishes high-probability, dimension-independent convergence guarantees for AdaGrad with heavy-ball momentum in non-convex stochastic optimization under generalized smoothness and gradient-noise assumptions.
